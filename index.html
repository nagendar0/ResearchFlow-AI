How is AI changing clinical trial operations in 2026?

Executive takeaway

AI is beginning to improve trial operations where organisations can connect high-quality data with accountable human decisions. The clearest near-term gains are practical: reducing protocol friction, identifying suitable sites, and matching patients faster.

What the evidence says

  • Operational uses show more mature evidence than fully autonomous clinical decision-making.
  • Successful programmes redesign workflows around AI rather than treating it as a standalone tool.
  • Validation and auditability remain essential for regulated deployment.
